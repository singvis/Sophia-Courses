Our Story

North Star Bakery started 10 years ago in a small kitchen. We wanted to bring fresh, handmade bread to our neighborhood. Today, we still use the same recipes and bake everything from scratch.

Sourcing Philosophy

We buy our flour from a local mill and our eggs from a farm down the road. We believe that good bread starts with good ingredients.

Staff Highlights

Our head baker, Sarah, has been with us since day one. She wakes up at 3 AM every day to start the ovens.
